Margin bridge tie-out

Rebuild a quarter's gross-profit bridge from ERP invoice lines, tie it to the ledger, and find the driver a favorable total hides. All data is made up: a parts maker with 6 product lines, 6 customers, 5,520 invoice lines and 36 rebate memos across two quarters.

The quarter in one line. Revenue rose from $29.05M to $31.44M, yet gross profit moved -$80K ($6.72M to $6.64M). The product-line bridge says surcharges barely stuck (price +$359K against a -$1.07M tariff hit) and mix helped (+$453K). The item-level bridge says the opposite: surcharges recovered 80% of the -$1.07M tariff hit (+$856K price), and a +$453K gain from richer product mix hid a -$540K drag from lower-margin installer (DIFM) business at the two largest retailers, which the product-line bridge booked as a price cut. The lever is the installer program's margin, not list price.

1. Tie-out first

Nothing is decomposed until the extract ties to the general ledger, each quarter: revenue within $1, and gross profit within $1 after named, sized items that post only at the ledger. Anything left unexplained stops the bridge.

Rebates post as separate customer-level memos, so they are allocated to invoice lines by gross sales within each customer and month before any price is calculated. Order types map to channels, and units convert to eaches, from one definitions file finance owns.

5. Go-live gate

25 planted scenarios (8 routine drivers, 8 hidden drivers, 6 bad extracts, 3 clean controls), scored with golive-gate. The gate: 24 of 25 overall, every hidden driver, every bad extract stopped, no false alarms, and zero critical failures (publishing on bad data, missing a planted data problem, or a bridge that does not tie).

AnalysisCases passedGateReport
Tie-out + item-level bridge + driver finder25 of 25GOevery case and check
Product-line workbook (ties to the ledger and stops if it does not; product line only, so it cannot see customer, channel, or origin; no data checks)7 of 25NO-GOevery case and check

The workbook's misses are mostly structural: a product-line view cannot name a customer or a channel, and without data checks it cannot name a bad extract. The cases were written on fixed data seeds; on seven seeds they were not written with, the tie-out passes 172 of 175 cases. CI runs the tests, this gate, and that sweep on every push.
