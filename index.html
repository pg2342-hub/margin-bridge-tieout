Margin bridge tie-out

Rebuild a quarter's gross-profit bridge from ERP invoice lines, tie it to the ledger, and find the driver a favorable total hides.

Ties to the ledger

Both quarters, revenue and gross profit, within $1 of the general ledger

The real driver is a -$540K drag from lower-margin installer (DIFM) business at the two largest retailers, hidden under a +$453K gain from richer product mix.

Surcharges recovered 80% of the -$1.07M tariff hit (+$856K price). The lever is the installer program's margin, not list price.

The product-line bridge says the opposite: surcharges barely stuck (price +$359K against a -$1.07M tariff hit) and mix helped (+$453K). It booked the installer shift as a price cut.

Rebates post as separate customer-level memos, so they are allocated to invoice lines by gross sales within each customer and month before any price is calculated. Order types map to channels, and units convert to eaches, from one definitions file finance owns.

What the driver finder flags

-$540K

Channel mix masked

Installer (DIFM) share grew inside product lines at Retailers A and B, hidden under a favorable product mix of +$453K.

+$856K

Price reattributed

Like-for-like price held up better than the product-line bridge shows (+$359K): it books the installer shift inside its lines as a price cut. Surcharges held.

-$1.07M

Tariff cost

All of it on China-origin parts; surcharges recovered 80%.

Go-live gate

25 planted scenarios (8 routine drivers, 8 hidden drivers, 6 bad extracts, 3 clean controls), scored with golive-gate. The gate: 24 of 25 overall, every hidden driver, every bad extract stopped, no false alarms, and zero critical failures (publishing on bad data, missing a planted data problem, or a bridge that does not tie).

The workbook's misses are mostly structural: a product-line view cannot name a customer or a channel, and without data checks it cannot name a bad extract. The cases were written on fixed data seeds; on seven seeds they were not written with, the tie-out passes 172 of 175 cases. CI runs the tests, this gate, and that sweep on every push.

Data

All data is made up: a parts maker with 6 product lines, 6 customers, 5,520 invoice lines and 36 rebate memos across two quarters. Customers, SKUs, prices, tariffs and the ledger are synthetic.
